Core Design Principles
🏃
Physical → Hippo Mood
Steps + custom habit completion drive Milo's mood on a 3-day rolling window. One bad day won't reset everything — the system is forgiving.
📖
Article → Mood Bonus + Library
One curated 10-min article daily. Reading it adds +0.5 to the mood score and counts toward Library & Reading Nook tile unlocks in the world map.
🗺
Hexagonal World Growth
Tiles are pointy-top hexagons. Steps unlock meadow + pond tiles. Streaks unlock forest. Articles unlock the library zone. Each tile hides an animal waiting to be met.
💬
Social → River Level
Pond water level is computed from last-message recency — no stored state. 10 days of silence = dry cracked ground. Resume chatting = river refills. Honest, never punishing.
✅
Custom Habits (Any Category)
Users define their own daily habits — physical, digital, mindfulness, social. All feed the mood engine equally. A screen-free morning counts as much as a gym session.
🌿
Calm, Not Gamified
No leaderboards, no punishing streak resets, no red badges. The hippo rests when you rest. The world waits. It's a companion and mirror — not a productivity game.
