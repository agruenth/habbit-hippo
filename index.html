Core Design Principles
🏃
Physical → Hippo Mood
Steps and custom habits drive the hippo's mood. Walk more, tick habits — Milo thrives. A 3-day rolling window means one bad day won't crash everything.
💬
Social → River Level
The pond water level is purely a function of chat recency. 10 days of silence = completely dry. Resume chatting = it refills. No gamification — just an honest mirror.
🗺
Habits → World Growth
Cumulative steps unlock meadow and pond tiles. Sustained streaks unlock the forest. Each tile brings a new animal encounter. The world grows as you do.
✅
Custom Habits
Beyond steps: journal, meditate, screen-free mornings, water intake. User-defined, manually checked. Digital wellbeing habits sit alongside physical ones with equal weight.
🔮
Never Stored, Always Fresh
Mood and river level are computed on the fly — never persisted. Improve your habits today and Milo reflects it immediately. Algorithmic improvements apply retroactively.
🌿
Calm, Not Gamified
No points, leaderboards, or punishing resets. The hippo rests when you rest. The pond dries gently. The world waits patiently. It's a companion, not a game.
